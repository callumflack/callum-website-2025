I work across changing problem spaces without losing the thread: make the hard system coherent, useful, and distinct. That currently means product exploration at Vana; it has also meant a high-stakes mobile workflow, a bi-directional knowledge system, fashion commerce, and a milk brand that remains on Australian shelves after twenty-five years.

Code is not downstream from my design work. An interface is made from state, routes, rendering, timing, and failure—not just pixels. Working in the medium lets me find the actual requirements, shorten the feedback loop, and make one coherent product instead of several approximations that happen to look alike.

<StoryPost slug="you-cant-design-a-ui-without-designing-the-code" />

At Cleared, that meant owning far more than a feature. I built the product from its first commit through to its sale as tech lead, designer, and frontend engineer, assembled a team of five, and connected a complex healthcare journey across quizzes, recommendations, identity, prescriptions, subscriptions, and checkout.

<StoryPost slug="cleared" />

Brand is also an interface, not a skin laid over a product: it gives a system its voice and shapes how people move through it. Kalaurie turns a fashion identity into a distinctive Shopify Hydrogen ecommerce experience. The Library of Economic Possibility carries a graphic brand system into a bi-directional research interface connecting reports, insights, sources, authors, and tags.

The judgment behind this work is not personal preference or last-mile polish. Taste is compressed knowhow: attention cultivated until it becomes a faster, more exact way of seeing a problem. Beauty matters for the same practical reason. People feel coherence before they can explain it; a beautiful thing earns the attention required to discover whether it can be trusted.

Writing is part of my design practice because names change what a team can notice and make. My current LLM work develops a vocabulary of owners, surfaces, burdens, proof, and oracles: language that keeps fast, fluent collaboration answerable to the product, source, route, or person that can say no.

<StoryPost slug="answerable-vocabulary-for-llm-work" />

The thread is broad attention joined to exact execution: carry the idea through language, interaction, and code until it becomes a working thing people can understand and trust.
